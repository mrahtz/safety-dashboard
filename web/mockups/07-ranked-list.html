Sort-First, Then Filter

A two-step flow — choose a benchmark first (which ranks models), then a ranked checklist lets you pick which models to include in the table.

Pros: Sort and select feel cohesive; ranking context aids selection.
Cons: Extra UI surface; two-step can feel heavy for quick views.
